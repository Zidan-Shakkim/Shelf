HOW SHELF WORKS

Shelf brings game discovery and personal tracking together in one place. Explore new games, organise your collection, and get recommendations based on what you enjoy playing.

DISCOVER

Explore a huge collection of games and discover something new to play. Search by title, genre or platform, browse popular releases, and explore detailed information about games that catch your attention.

ORGANISE

Keep all your games in one place and organise them based on your gaming progress. Add games to your collection and move them between To Play, Playing, and Played whenever your status changes.

FIND WHAT'S NEXT

Shelf uses the games in your collection to understand what you enjoy. Discover recommendations based on genres, tags, platforms and your favourite games, helping you find something new when you don't know what to play.
